❤️ The Human Heart

Location: Chest, between the lungs.

Function: Pumps blood around the body.

Structure: Four chambers: two atria and two ventricles.

Importance: Supplies tissues with oxygen and nutrients.

🧠 The Brain

Location: Inside the skull.

Function: Coordinates movement, memory, thinking, and senses.

Major regions: Cerebrum, cerebellum, and brainstem.

🫁 The Lungs

Location: Chest cavity.

Function: Exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide.

Important structure: Alveoli, where gas exchange occurs.

The Liver

Location: Upper right side of the abdomen.

Function: Processes nutrients, produces bile, and helps remove harmful substances.

The Kidneys

Location: Back of the abdominal cavity.

Function: Filter blood, regulate fluid balance, and produce urine.

Basic unit: Nephron.

The Stomach

Location: Upper left side of the abdomen.

Function: Stores food and helps break it down using acid and enzymes.
